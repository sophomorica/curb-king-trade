Not a franchise

Buy the equipment. Run your own curbing business.

You are not buying a territory, a brand license, or a royalty plan. After the sale the equipment is yours, and so is the business.

  • Start with the starter$14,600 for a machine, mixer, edger, and three slipforms.
  • Grow into morePackages from $15,000 to $50,000. Add crews and equipment as work comes in.
  • FinancingIn-house financing for qualified buyers, and work with banks and leasing companies.
  • Small crewThe business can run with as few as two people.

How to get started

  1. Call 1-800-YES-CURB and say whether you are starting fresh or adding to what you own.
  2. Pick a package and a financing option, if you need one.
  3. Book training: a day on a real job with a crew, then a day one-on-one.
  4. Take delivery or pick up in Brigham City, and start pouring.

Earnings depend on your market, your prices, and how much you work. Ask the shop for their white paper on the curbing business.
